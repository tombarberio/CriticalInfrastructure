Module 2 · Introduction

Introduction to Critical Infrastructure Protection

Critical infrastructure represents the backbone of our national way of life — the systems and assets whose disruption or destruction would have devastating consequences for public safety, security, and the economy.

This module introduces the foundational concepts, key terminology, and strategic framework that underpin the protection of critical infrastructure in the United States. You will explore how government, the private sector, and citizens share responsibility for keeping these systems secure.

Part 3 · Presidential Mandate

The Call to Action

Following the September 11, 2001 attacks, President George W. Bush issued the National Strategy for the Physical Protection of Critical Infrastructures and Key Assets — the first comprehensive federal framework for CIP.

▸ Catalyst for Action

The 9/11 attacks demonstrated the extent of U.S. vulnerability to the terrorist threat and prompted firm national resolve to protect critical infrastructure and key assets from further exploitation.

▸ A Shared Responsibility

The strategy established that protection of critical infrastructure is not solely a government responsibility. Infrastructure owners and operators, state and local governments, federal agencies, and private citizens all play an essential role.

▸ Whole-of-Nation Approach

The Office of Homeland Security worked with public and private entities to implement the Homeland Security Advisory System and coordinate protective actions across all levels of government and critical sectors.

Part 4 · Strategic Framework

Goals, Principles & the Road Ahead

▸ Clear Goals and Objectives

The National Strategy identified a clear set of goals and outlined guiding principles to secure infrastructures vital to public health and safety, national security, governance, the economy, and public confidence.

▸ Defining Roles and Responsibilities

The strategy provided a unifying structure — defining roles for federal departments and agencies, state and municipal governments, private-sector operators, research institutions, and professional associations nationwide.

▸ A Determined Adversary

The strategy acknowledged that the terrorist enemy is highly determined, patient, and adaptive. Protecting critical infrastructure requires that the nation remain united in resolve, tenacious in approach, and harmonious in action.

▸ Foundation for Cooperation

Most importantly, the strategy established a foundation for building a cooperative environment in which government, industry, and private citizens can work together to protect the infrastructures and assets that are the basis of American daily life.
